Your structural advantage
The rare combination: Civil engineering + software + AI is genuinely uncommon. Most ML engineers cannot reason about physical systems. Most civil engineers cannot build software. You are building toward a position where you can do both — digital twins, structural simulation acceleration, engineering AI, FEM optimization. These domains have far less competition than generic "AI apps" and command significantly higher salaries.
Pacing guidance: This tab is background enrichment, not a sprint. Allocate 5–8% of weekly time (~3–5 hrs/week). Choose one track at a time. Priority order: Computer Architecture → Signal Processing → Control Theory → Numerical Methods → GPU Computing.
